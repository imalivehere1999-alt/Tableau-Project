## 🎯 Business question
How can a retail team use interactive reporting to understand **sales, profit, quantity and regional performance**?

## 🔎 What this project explores
- Sales and profit KPIs for high-level performance monitoring
- Product- and region-level comparisons
- Interactive filters to focus on specific segments
- Visual patterns that help identify where further investigation is needed

## 🧰 Technology
**Tableau Desktop** · Calculated fields · Visual dashboards · Interactive filtering

## 📁 Explore the workbook
Open [`My Tableu Project.twb`](./My%20Tableu%20Project.twb) to inspect the Tableau workbook. To view it interactively, open it with Tableau Desktop and ensure its underlying data connections are available.

## 💡 What I would ask stakeholders
1. Which regions contribute the most sales and profit?
2. Where do strong sales coexist with weak profitability?
3. Which segments deserve deeper investigation?
